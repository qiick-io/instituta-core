# Brand voice

## Northwind's voice

- Plain, warm and direct. Short sentences.
- Say what happened and what to do next; no jargon, no hype.

## Examples

- Instead of "An unexpected error has occurred", write "We couldn't save your changes. Try again in a minute."
- Client brains can override this voice: see each client's brand file.
