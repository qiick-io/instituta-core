# Glossary

## Terms

- **Brain**: the shared knowledge Northwind and its agents read before working on a project.
- **Client brain**: the part of the brain that belongs to one client; it overrides the handbook.
- **Proposal**: a change to the brain suggested by a person or an agent, merged only after review.
- **Staged rollout**: see [[Staged rollouts for client apps]].
